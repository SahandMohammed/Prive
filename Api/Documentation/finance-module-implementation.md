# LogicBloom ERP: Financial Accounting System MVP

## Overview
We have transitioned from a simple MoneyBox model to a **Strict Double-Entry Bookkeeping System** strictly based on **IFRS Standards**. Every financial movement in the system now generates a balanced Journal Entry where `Total Debit = Total Credit`. 

## 1. Core Architecture & Double-Entry Engine

### Chart of Accounts (CoA)
The `AccountEntity` is structured as a hierarchical tree (Parent/Child accounts). It supports the 5 core IFRS account types:
- **1000 - Assets** (Cash/Bank Treasuries, Accounts Receivable, Inventory)
- **2000 - Liabilities** (Accounts Payable, Tax Liabilities)
- **3000 - Equity** (Owner's Equity, Retained Earnings)
- **4000 - Revenue** (Sales Revenue, Exchange Gains)
- **5000 - Expenses** (Operating Expenses, Exchange Losses)

Only leaf accounts (`IsLeaf = true`) can accept Journal Entries.

### Multi-Currency Engine
- Uses `CurrencyEntity`.
- Supports a Base Currency and Foreign Currencies. 
- Realized Exchange Gains/Losses are automated via ledger entries.

### The Ledger
- **`JournalEntryEntity` & `JournalEntryLineEntity`**: The immutable ledger of truth. All Invoices and Vouchers post their debits and credits here using Base and Transaction currency amounts.

## 2. Entities & Sub-Ledgers
- **`ContactEntity`**: Represents `Customers` (linked to A/R) and `Vendors` (linked to A/P). 
- Running balances are generated by querying the sub-ledger (the specific `AccountEntity` tied to the Contact).

## 3. Invoicing Module
- **`InvoiceEntity` & `InvoiceLineEntity`**: Handles Sales and Purchases separately.
- **Frontend Segregation**: Sales Invoices are managed under the **Sales** module. Purchase Invoices are managed under the **Purchases** module. 
- Dynamic Status: Paid/Unpaid/Debt statuses are computed on-the-fly based on Voucher Allocations.

## 4. Treasury, Cash Flow & Financial Vouchers
- Safes and Banks are now standard Asset Accounts.
- **`VoucherEntity` & `VoucherAllocationEntity`**: Handles Receipts, Payments, Internal Transfers, and Currency Exchange. Vouchers are allocated to specific Invoices to mark them as paid.

## 5. UI / Frontend Structure (Feature-Sliced)
The frontend sidebar features a clean nested accordion hierarchy:
- **Sales Module**
  - Sales Invoice (`/sales/invoices`): Dedicated form to select Customers and build line items with custom revenue account mapping.
  - Sales Return Invoice (`/sales/returns`): Manage returns and credit notes.
  - POS (`/sales/pos`): Point of Sale terminal cashier register.
- **Purchase Module**
  - Purchase Invoice (`/purchases/invoices`): Record bills from suppliers and link items to inventory/expense accounts.
  - Purchase Return Invoice (`/purchases/returns`): Manage supplier returns and debit notes.
- **Accounting Module**
  - Chart of Accounts (`/accounting/chart`): Hierarchical tree listing categories, sub-categories, and leaf accounts.
  - Journal Entries (`/accounting/journal`): General ledger entries with precise Debit and Credit line breakdowns.
  - Currencies (`/accounting/currencies`): Currency rate adjustments and base currency control.
- **Finance Module**
  - Treasury (Safes) (`/finance/treasury`): Cashier registers, safes, and bank asset accounts.
  - Vouchers (`/finance/vouchers`): Receipts (customer payments) and Payments (vendor payments) with invoice debt allocation features.
  - Internal Transfer (`/finance/transfers`): Safe transfers.
